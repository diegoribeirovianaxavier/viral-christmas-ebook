# Christmas Dinner Feast: 40 Showstopping Holiday Recipes 🎄✨

An editorial-grade, publication-ready Christmas Dinner culinary e-book featuring 40 viral holiday dinner recipes in native American English. Formatted for high-resolution US Letter print (HTML5/CSS3 Print) and compiled into PDF.

---

## 📖 Structure & Chapters

- **Chapter 1: Festive Dinner Starters & Appetizers** (Recipes 01–08)
- **Chapter 2: Showstopping Christmas Roasts & Main Courses** (Recipes 09–16)
- **Chapter 3: Classic & Viral Holiday Side Dishes** (Recipes 17–24)
- **Chapter 4: Holiday Stuffings, Dinner Rolls, Salads & Sauces** (Recipes 25–32)
- **Chapter 5: Grand Finale Christmas Dinner Desserts** (Recipes 33–40)

---

## 🛠️ Project Contents

- `recipes_data.json`: Complete dataset containing all 40 curated Christmas Dinner recipes with precise US and metric measurements, times, yields, step-by-step instructions, and chef's tips.
- `ebook.html`: 50-page print layout (Cover, Dedication & Conversion Table, Table of Contents, 5 Chapter Dividers, 40 Recipe Pages, Back Cover).
- `style.css`: Professional print styling tailored for 8.5in x 11in pages, typographic hierarchy, and custom festive color palette (`#FAF7F2`, `#1B3022`, `#8B1E24`, `#C5A059`).
- `assets/`: Editorial food photography assets generated exclusively for each dish.
- `verify_images.js`: Cryptographic SHA-256 validation script ensuring 100% unique visual assets.
- `compile_pdf.js`: Headless browser print compiler for high-resolution PDF generation.
